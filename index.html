Why does one quote say ₹50,000, another ₹90,000 and a third ₹1.25 lakh for the same product?

Usually the quotes cover different work: one is “make it work on my table”, another is “make it ready to sell”. Ask every vendor what is included, what is not, and what is left before you can sell.

Read the full note →
Should an old PIC or STM8 product move to a low-cost 8051?

Often yes, and without a 32-bit redesign: the product can stay 8-bit and 5 V, with no level shifters and no full firmware rewrite.
