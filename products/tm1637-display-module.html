TM1637 6-Digit LED Display & Button Module, a ready HMI

6-digit 7-segment LED display with 8 integrated tactile buttons. Driven via just 2 MCU pins (CLK + DIO) using onboard scan logic. Includes 5V supply header (J4) for powering auxiliary boards, and (D1) Power LED indicator. We provide Embedded C driver files to help with coding.
