Nuvoton MG51 (MG51FB9AE) development board.

A development board for the Nuvoton MG51 (MG51FB9AE), a 1T 8051 microcontroller, with full I/O breakout on 2.54 mm headers. Programs over the 4-pin ICP header with Nuvoton Nu-Link, and ships ready for the free SDCC toolchain.
